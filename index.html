Frequently Asked Questions

What is included with each puppy? ▼
Each puppy includes AKC registration papers, health certifications, a health guarantee, microchipping, and initial vaccinations. You'll also receive lifetime support and our puppy practice program guide.
How quickly can I get my puppy? ▼
Puppies can be delivered within 2-8 hours domestically across the USA, and within 48 hours internationally with proper arrangements. Express delivery options are available.
Do you offer a health guarantee? ▼
Yes! We offer a comprehensive health guarantee covering genetic conditions for the first 2 years. If any health issues arise, we provide full support and refund or replacement options.
What is your refund policy? ▼
Full refunds are available within 30 days if you're not satisfied. The $300 documentation fee is non-refundable. Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days. See our Delivery page for complete terms.
Are your puppies AKC registered? ▼
Yes, all our puppies are purebred English Cocker Spaniels with full AKC registration. Documentation is provided upon delivery.
Do you offer lifetime support? ▼
Absolutely! We provide lifetime support including health advice, training tips, behavior guidance, and access to our private community. We're here for you and your puppy every step of the way.
