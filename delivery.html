Our Policies

Health Assurance & Guarantee ▼

Coverage: We provide a comprehensive 2-year health guarantee covering genetic conditions and hereditary diseases.

What's Covered:

  • Hip dysplasia
  • Elbow dysplasia
  • Genetic eye conditions
  • Heart defects
  • Other hereditary conditions

What's Not Covered: Injuries, accidents, improper care, and conditions resulting from negligence.

Refund Conditions ▼

Full Refund Eligible:

  • Genetic health issues diagnosed within 2 years
  • Serious congenital defects
  • Puppy not meeting health standards upon arrival
  • Change of mind within 30 days

Refund Process: Contact us immediately if you wish to apply for a refund. We'll work with you to resolve the issue or process the refund.

Non-Refundable Fees ▼

Documentation Fee ($300): This fee is always non-refundable, regardless of circumstances. It covers:

  • Complete paperwork preparation
  • Health certifications
  • AKC registration documents
  • Delivery coordination
  • Administrative costs

Note: If a puppy is unavailable due to unforeseen circumstances, we'll offer a replacement puppy or full refund of the puppy price only (documentation fee retained).

Refund Timeline ▼

Processing Time: All approved refunds are processed within 5-10 business days.

Refund Method: Refunds are issued to the original payment method used during purchase.

Replacement Option: Instead of a refund, you may choose a replacement puppy from a future litter at no additional cost.

Lifetime Support ▼

We provide lifetime support to all puppy families including:

  • 24/7 health and behavioral advice
  • Training guidance and tips
  • Nutritional recommendations
  • Socialization strategies
  • Emergency support (even after purchase)
  • Access to our private community

No question is too small — we're committed to your puppy's lifelong health and happiness!

Delivery FAQ

How much does delivery cost? ▼
Delivery costs are included in the puppy price. No additional shipping fees apply for domestic USA deliveries. International shipping can be arranged separately at cost.
Can I pick up my puppy instead? ▼
Yes! Local pickups can be arranged. Please contact us to discuss pickup options and available dates.
What if my puppy arrives and I'm not happy? ▼
We have a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not satisfied for any reason, contact us immediately. We'll work with you to find a solution, whether that's training support, a replacement, or a refund.
Are puppies insured during delivery? ▼
Yes! All puppies are fully insured during transit. We use safe, climate-controlled carriers and track every delivery in real-time to ensure your puppy arrives safely.
