Common Questions

How quickly will you respond to my message? ▼
We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours. During business hours, we usually respond much faster! For urgent matters, call us directly or use WhatsApp for immediate contact.
Can I schedule a video call to see the puppies? ▼
Absolutely! We offer video consultations so you can meet your potential puppy before purchase. Contact us to schedule a video call at a convenient time for both of us.
What's the best way to contact you? ▼
The fastest method is WhatsApp or a direct call. For detailed questions, email works great. Our contact form also works well for inquiries you'd like us to consider carefully.
Do you offer consultations before purchase? ▼
Yes! We encourage consultations. We want to make sure each puppy goes to the right home. Feel free to ask us anything about a specific puppy, care requirements, or our breeding program.
Can I get updates after I reserve a puppy? ▼
Of course! After you reserve a puppy, we'll send you regular updates including photos, videos, and developmental milestones. You'll be part of your puppy's journey from day one!
