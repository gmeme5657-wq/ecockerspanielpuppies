Our Story

Why We Started

Over 8 years ago, we fell in love with English Cocker Spaniels and their incredible temperament. Their loyalty, intelligence, and boundless affection inspired us to create a breeding program dedicated to producing healthy, well-socialized puppies for families.

Our mission is simple: raise puppies with care, love, and dedication, ensuring each one receives the best possible start in life and is ready to bring joy to their forever homes.

Every puppy born through our program is treated as a beloved family member from day one. We believe that early socialization and proper care create the foundation for happy, healthy, and well-behaved companions.

Our Breeding Process

1. Parent Selection

We carefully select healthy, well-tempered parents with excellent pedigrees and health screening.

2. Health Screening

All breeding dogs undergo complete health screening including genetic testing and veterinary clearance.

3. Early Development

Puppies are raised in our home with early socialization, handling, and exposure to various experiences.

4. Veterinary Care

Regular vet checkups, vaccinations, deworming, and microchipping. Complete health documentation provided.

5. Training & Preparation

Puppies receive basic training including crate training, potty training, and socialization preparation.

6. Safe Delivery & Support

Puppies are safely transported with health guarantee and lifetime support from our team.

Common Questions

How long have you been breeding? ▼
We've been dedicated to breeding premium English Cocker Spaniels for over 8 years. Our experience helps us produce healthy, well-tempered puppies consistently.
Are your puppies registered? ▼
Yes! All our puppies are AKC (American Kennel Club) registered and come with official registration documents.
What vaccinations do puppies receive? ▼
Puppies receive age-appropriate vaccinations, deworming, flea/tick prevention, and are microchipped before delivery. A full health record is provided.
Do you provide health guarantees? ▼
Yes! We offer a comprehensive 2-year health guarantee covering genetic conditions. If any health issues arise, we provide full support and replacement or refund options.
Can I visit your facility? ▼
We value transparency and welcome inquiries. Contact us to arrange a visit or arrange a video call to meet our breeding program and see our puppies.
